Follow one signal across a glutamate synapse: electrical in the presynaptic neuron, chemical across the cleft, electrical again in the next cell, with the astrocyte cleaning up. Built from Lectures 4–9 of Neural Transmission & Metabolism.

electrical→Ca²⁺ signal→chemical (glutamate)→electrical (EPSP)→clean-up

Difficult terms in this step

A simplified teaching model: real terminals contain hundreds of vesicles and proteins, and one EPSP from a single synapse is usually far too small to reach threshold. Concentrations follow the Lecture 9 slide: Ca²⁺ ≈ 100 nM inside, ≈ 1 mM outside.
