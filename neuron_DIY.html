Parts of a Neuron

A neuron is an information-processing cell.

Structure of Neuron

  • Dendrites: Branching extensions that receive chemical signals from other neurons and convert them into small electrical changes.
  • Soma: The cell body, which contains the nucleus and keeps the cell alive; it combines the incoming signals from the dendrites.
  • Axon hillock: The cone-shaped region where the axon leaves the soma; if the combined signal here passes a threshold, it triggers an action potential.
  • Axon: A long, thin fiber that carries the action potential away from the soma toward other cells.
  • Myelin: A fatty sheath wrapped around the axon that insulates it and makes signals travel much faster, jumping between gaps called nodes of Ranvier.
  • Axon terminals: The branched ends of the axon, which release neurotransmitters across the synapse to pass the signal to the next cell.
